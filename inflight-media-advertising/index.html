Formats and environments

Choose the format for the audience and objective.

Inflight media can bring a brand into the passenger experience through personal screens, shared cabin environments and editorial content. The mix depends on the airline, route, inventory and campaign brief.

Seatback screens and inflight entertainment

Video and other placements on personal screens can give a message a prominent place in the onboard experience. Opportunities and specifications vary by airline and system.

Overhead monitors

Shared cabin screens can carry video messages across a flight. The available placements, programming and audience delivery are confirmed with the relevant airline partner.

Inflight magazines

Print titles offer a longer-form environment for brand storytelling and editorial adjacency. Circulation, routes, language and booking details depend on the publication.

Portals and connected cabin media

Where available, onboard portals and connectivity environments can add digital touchpoints. Targeting, interaction and reporting options vary by platform.

Plan across the journey

Reach travellers before departure and onboard.

Inflight advertising can work as part of a broader travel media plan. AIM brings together opportunities across travel, business, luxury, digital and out-of-home media, helping brands consider where and when to connect with travellers. Each recommendation should reflect the campaign objective, the media available in that market and the information the partner can substantiate.

Airport, lounge, publisher and outdoor options can extend a campaign towards departure, while airline media can continue the conversation onboard. These opportunities are market- and partner-specific, so AIM confirms the current offer before a proposal is made.

Frequently asked questions

Inflight media advertising, explained.

What is inflight media advertising?

It is advertising delivered through media in an aircraft cabin, including formats such as seatback entertainment, overhead monitors and inflight magazines. The specific media available differs by airline.

Can campaigns be planned around routes or markets?

Route and market options depend on each partner’s inventory and booking terms. AIM can check the available choices against your audience, campaign timing and objectives.

Can inflight media be combined with other travel media?

Yes, where suitable opportunities are available. AIM can consider selected digital, publisher, airport, lounge and out-of-home media alongside airline formats to connect with travellers at different points in the journey.

What information should I include in a campaign enquiry?

Share your campaign objective, audience, markets or routes, preferred timing and any format requirements. AIM will follow up to discuss suitable opportunities and confirm details with the relevant media partners.
