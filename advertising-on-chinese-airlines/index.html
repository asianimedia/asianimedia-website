China airline media

How can an international brand advertise on Chinese airlines?

Start with the travellers you want to reach, where they are flying and when. AIM can compare Air China, China Eastern, China Southern and Hainan Airlines, check availability with the airlines and prepare a proposal for your brand or media agency.

Which airline fits my audience?

For a destination campaign, start with passengers travelling from your priority markets. For a premium product, tell us whether cabin class matters. An airline’s total passenger figure gives a sense of scale. The audience for a particular placement depends on the routes, aircraft and format included. Compare the four airlines and their media profiles.

Can I buy a specific route or cabin?

Tell us which routes or cabins are essential. AIM will check whether the airline can sell that coverage separately and identify any wider network coverage included in the price.

How do the formats compare?

Seatback video reaches passengers who select eligible entertainment on a personal screen. Overhead video uses shared cabin monitors. An airline magazine gives readers room to explore a destination or product in more detail. We check format availability for each airline and campaign period.

What determines pricing?

The airline, format, coverage and length of the booking shape the quotation. Share your indicative budget and currency so we can compare options on the same basis. International advertisers can request a USD quotation valid for 30 days.

What campaign reporting can I expect?

Tell us what your team needs to report before booking. AIM will check which delivery records, placement evidence and audience estimates each airline can provide. Passenger totals alone cannot establish how many people saw an advertisement.

How might a tourism board use the comparison?

For example, a tourism board promoting winter travel could brief AIM on mainland departure cities, its destination and booking season. We would compare route-specific options with any wider network buy, then show the cost and available delivery evidence for each. The board could decide how much wider coverage is worth for that brief.
